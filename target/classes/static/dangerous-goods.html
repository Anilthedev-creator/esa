Dangerous Goods
Storage & Handling

Complete compliance solutions for hazardous chemicals. We align your facility's operational frameworks with strict Australian AS1940 standards to guarantee safety and compliance.

AS1940 Compliance and Auditing

Managing industrial chemical stores requires absolute adherence to legislative benchmarks. Our process services safeguard your personnel, assets, and the local ecosystem from potential chemical hazards.

Led by our Principal Chemical Engineer, Elizabeth Harangozo (former Chair of the Engineers Australia College of Chemical Engineering), our division applies industry-leading methodologies to design robust management strategies.

Our Core Core Focus

• Combustible Liquid Storage Layouts
• Bunding Capacity Calculations
• Vent System Safety Verifications
• Separation Distance Boundary Audits

Our Risk Management Framework

1. Classification Audit

We accurately cross-reference your chemical database inventory against the Australian Dangerous Goods (ADG) index parameters.

2. Containment Layout

Engineering custom secondary spill containment layouts and physical fire-rated partition segregation solutions.

3. Certification Sign-off

Compiling comprehensive asset compliance registries and providing endorsed structural sign-off documentation.
