EFFICIENCY & SAVINGS

Utility Consumption
Appraisals

Optimize your facility’s performance. Our engineering-led appraisals identify hidden energy losses and provide data-driven pathways to minimize consumption and costs.

Our Appraisal Methodology

1. Inventory & Baseline

We create a comprehensive list of all energy-consuming equipment—from production machinery to HVAC systems—and collect historical data to establish your current usage baseline.

2. Measurement & Analysis

Using high-precision flowmeters and energy balancers, we quantify thermal losses and leaks in steam, water, and compressed air distribution systems.

3. Optimization Roadmap

We benchmark your data against industry best practices and deliver a prioritized plan for equipment upgrades or process changes to return spend to your operations budget.

Why Assess Your Utilities?

  • 💰 Cost Reduction: Identify "quick win" opportunities to reduce monthly utility costs by 10-25%.
  • 🔧 Reliability: Uncover equipment risks before they lead to unexpected downtime.
  • 🌍 Decarbonization: Lower your carbon footprint by minimizing energy waste per unit of production.

The ESA Edge

Unlike basic audits, our engineering appraisals use Dynamic Plant Data. We don't just look at bills; we model individual unit operations to ensure every recommendation is technically and financially sound.
