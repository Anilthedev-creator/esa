OPERATIONAL READINESS

Workplace Health, Safety & Environment (WHSE)

Safety is part of planning and carrying out engineering work. The controls needed depend on the site, the task and the hazards present. This page describes a general approach; it is not a substitute for a site's own safety plan or current legal advice.

Our approach

Work should be planned with the client and site team. Anyone involved should raise hazards, follow site instructions and stop work when it is not safe to continue.

Plan the work

Before site tasks begin, identify the work scope, hazards, permits, equipment and people involved. Use the site's risk assessment and job safety processes where they apply.

Follow site and legal requirements

Requirements differ between sites and tasks. The project team should check the current legislation, standards, client rules and environmental conditions that apply to the work before starting.

Control hazardous energy

Where work involves equipment or hazardous energy, agree isolation and lock-out/tag-out arrangements with the site owner before work begins. Follow the site's approved procedure.
