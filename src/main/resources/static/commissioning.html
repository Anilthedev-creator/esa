Plant Commissioning &
Operational Readiness

Bridging the gap between construction and production. We help teams check equipment and systems against approved designs and agreed handover requirements.

Request a commissioning review Speak with an engineer

Planning for a Clear Handover

Commissioning work can include reviewing the agreed scope, checking available records, coordinating tests and recording open items. The tasks depend on the equipment, site procedures and approvals in place.

  • ✓ Review the agreed handover requirements.
  • ✓ Coordinate inspections and tests with the site team.
  • ✓ Record findings and items that still need action.

Possible deliverables

Depending on the agreed scope, deliverables may include checklists, test records, a handover summary and a list of open items.

Typical Commissioning Tasks

01

Early Planning

Agreeing scope, responsibilities, timing and site requirements with the project team.

02

Pre-Commissioning

Reviewing installation records and coordinating walkdowns or checks that are approved for the site and included in the agreed scope.

03

Functional Testing

Coordinating functional tests with the site team and recording results under the approved procedures.

04

Performance Verification

Recording available operating results against agreed criteria. Outcomes depend on the plant, operating conditions and data available.
