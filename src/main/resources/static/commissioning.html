Ensuring a Safe Handover

Plant commissioning is the integrated application of engineering techniques to check and test every operational component of a project. From individual instruments to complex amalgamations like modules and subsystems, we verify that everything functions according to the final client's requirements.

  • ✓ Reliability: Reducing downtime and long-term maintenance costs.
  • ✓ Safety: Strict adherence to audits and risk assessments.
  • ✓ Compliance: Ensuring all regulatory standards are met before startup.

Why Commission with ESA?

We deliver documented records of functionality for future reference, prioritizing efficiency to optimize your project timeline and prevent costly delays.

Our Proven Commissioning Process

01

Early Planning

Defining scope, timelines, and safety protocols. Early planning allows us to treat commissioning as a core phase, not an afterthought.

02

Pre-Commissioning

Often called "Static Commissioning," we verify installations through walkthroughs, cleaning, and pressure testing before systems are energized.

03

Functional Testing

Live equipment testing. We start up components, calibrate controls, and verify system integration and interlocks.

04

Performance Verification

Proving the plant meets KPIs like efficiency and throughput under real operating conditions to give stakeholders confidence.
