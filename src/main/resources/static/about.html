OUR STORY

Two Decades of
Technical Excellence.

Established in October 2004, Engineering Services Australia has grown from strength to strength over 22 years of operation. We are a consulting firm dedicated to servicing rural, regional, and metropolitan Australia from our primary base in North East Victoria.

Our philosophy is built on the principles of technical excellence and attention to detail. We believe in complete honesty and transparency, fostering trusted relationships with clients across the manufacturing, mining, fuel, and energy sectors.

Our Territory

While based in Victoria, we service clients in Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, and remote sites across the Northern Territory and Western Australia.

Our Leadership
