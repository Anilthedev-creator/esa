Reviewing Dangerous Goods Storage

Dangerous goods storage and handling must be assessed for the materials, quantities, facility and local requirements involved. A site review can help identify questions and actions for the responsible team.

The team can review available site information and discuss options with the people responsible for dangerous goods at the facility.

A review is not a blanket compliance guarantee or certification. Requirements and responsibility depend on the site and the scope of work.

Possible review topics

• Storage and handling arrangements
• Secondary containment
• Ventilation and separation
• Site records and procedures

Possible Review Steps

1. Review information

Review the materials, quantities, site documents and requirements that apply to the agreed scope.

2. Consider controls

Discuss practical storage, containment and separation options with the responsible site team.

3. Record findings

Document agreed findings and note where additional specialist assessment or certification may be needed.
