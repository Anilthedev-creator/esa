Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information the ESA Engineering website asks for and how this version of the site handles it. It is a plain-language website notice, not legal advice. ESA Engineering should confirm that it matches its day-to-day practices before relying on it as a final privacy policy.

Information you provide

If you create an account, book a service or send an enquiry, the site may store your name, email address, phone number, company name, booking details and messages. The contact form can also save an optional PDF, JPG or PNG attachment of up to 3 MB. New passwords are stored as hashes, not readable text. Older Node data is converted on startup; an older Java record is converted after a successful sign-in.

How it is used and stored

The information is used to manage accounts, enquiries, bookings and payment records. Depending on the selected backend, records are stored in a database or local data files; attachments are stored with the server data. The hosting provider and storage location depend on deployment and should be confirmed by the site operator. This version does not automatically remove old enquiries or attachments, so ESA should set and follow a retention schedule. When you sign in, the browser stores an access token in local storage for up to 12 hours or until you sign out. Avoid staying signed in on a shared device.

Payments and email

The built-in card form is a demo only. It does not charge a card; do not enter real card details. In demo mode, the server checks the test details and stores only the card's last four digits. Bank-transfer references are recorded for staff to check. A real payment provider has not been connected.

The app can send an enquiry acknowledgement to you and a notice to ESA when the site operator configures an SMTP email service. If SMTP is not configured or delivery fails, the server saves a private copy in its local outbox instead. The form tells you whether email was sent or saved; an outbox copy is not an email delivered to your inbox. The operator must confirm the email provider, server location and retention period for that outbox.

Website services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and shows an OpenStreetMap map on the contact page. Your browser connects to those services to load the fonts or map, so they may receive basic request information such as your IP address and browser details under their own policies. The site has no advertising or analytics tracker configured in this code.

Access and questions

Please contact ESA Engineering to ask about your information, correct it or request its removal. Email engsa@live.com.au or call 0402 464 823. The site does not yet provide an automatic deletion or download tool.

Keeping information safe

Access to the admin tools requires an administrator account. This code alone cannot promise security for every deployment. The operator must use HTTPS, protect the server and its backups, set strong secrets and credentials, limit staff access, and turn off demo features before handling real customer information.
