Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Engineering Services Australia Pty Ltd ("ESA Engineering") balances operational visibility with rigorous client data protections. This Privacy Policy details how our organization collects, manages, and safeguards sensitive data variables in full alignment with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) outlined under the Privacy Act 1988.

1. Information Collection Scope

We securely collect identity metrics provided via our lead-capture systems (such as corporate names, email pathways, and site telephone references) alongside technical site schematics required to execute requested utility appraisals or commissioning reviews.

2. Data Utilization Models

All extracted metrics are utilized strictly to construct technical engineering estimates, refine platform logic integrations, or optimize local resource allocation. ESA Engineering strictly bars the external trade, sale, or distribution of client information sets to unauthorized commercial parties.

3. Industrial Infrastructure Security

Client operational logs and technical system files are contained behind high-tier cryptographic barriers and access-controlled enterprise frameworks to mitigate the threat of digital breaches or unauthorized modification.
