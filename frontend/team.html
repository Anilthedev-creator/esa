Our Leadership

ESA is led by senior engineers with more than 60 combined years of experience across mechanical, chemical and project delivery — equally at home in the office as on a remote site.

GH

Greg Howard

BE (Mechanical) · Director & Principal Engineer

With over 30 years of experience, Greg has worked in almost every field of mechanical engineering — pressure vessels and piping, materials handling, dust and pollution control, high-speed automated equipment, minerals processing, power generation (coal & hydro), hazardous goods, pipelines, dams, bridges, water & waste water, roads, tram and rail, tank farms and the petrochemical industry.

Within each specialty Greg has assisted with design, maintenance, inspection, condition assessment, fault finding, equipment and plant modification, and commissioning. In a business support function he has provided feasibility & definitive studies, estimation, project surveillance, works supervision, project engineering, project management, constructability reviews and third-party reviews — a rare breed equally at home in the office as on-site, known for clever solutions to complex problems.

EH

Elizabeth Harangozo

MIEAust · General Manager & Principal Chemical Engineer

Elizabeth specialises in Dangerous Goods compliance and process engineering. She has served as Chair of the Engineers Australia College of Chemical Engineering and brings deep expertise in storage, handling and regulatory compliance for hazardous materials.

As General Manager she leads ESA's client engagements end-to-end — from feasibility and estimation through to delivery, supervision and commissioning — ensuring every project is delivered safely, on time and to budget.
